Turn on the time-savers (optional)

When you trust the results, these options in Sources → Media Intake remove clicks:

OptionWhat it saves you
Automatically watch these folders for new filesNew downloads appear in the Queue by themselves.
Automatically approve high-confidence naming matchesSkips the Naming Review sheet when the online title matches the file name exactly.
Automatically merge multi-file releases with a clear sequenceSkips the Merge Review sheet when parts are numbered and compatible.
Remove empty folders automaticallyTidies folders left behind. Items go to the Trash.
Clean up processed filesMoves the original parts to the Trash after a Queue merge or conversion. Off by default so your originals stay as a backup. Does not apply to files saved from the editor.
